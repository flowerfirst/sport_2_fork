## 1. Introduction

### What is OCULUS SPORT?

OCULUS SPORT is a comprehensive campus sports facilities booking application designed specifically for UTS (University) students and staff. The app provides a seamless way to browse, book, and manage reservations for various on-campus sports facilities.

### Key Features

- **Easy Authentication**: Secure login and sign-up process
- **Real-time Booking**: Check availability and book facilities instantly
- **Multiple Sports**: Access to various sports facilities including badminton, basketball, football, pickleball, and more
- **Schedule Management**: View and manage your upcoming bookings
- **Event Updates**: Stay informed about the latest sports events on campus
- **Booking History**: Track all your past and current bookings
- **Price Transparency**: View pricing information for all facilities
- **Offline Access**: Access your profile and booking history even without internet

### System Requirements

- **Platform**: Android (API 21+) or Windows 10+
- **Storage**: Minimum 50 MB free space
- **Internet**: Required for booking and real-time updates (offline mode available for viewing history and profile)
- **Account**: Valid UTS email address for registration

## 5. Booking Sports Facilities

### Browsing Available Facilities

1. **Go to Book Now (Home Tab)**
   - Tap the center "Book Now" tab at the bottom

2. **View Categories**
   - Scroll to see sport categories (Badminton, Basketball, Football, etc.)
   - Tap a category to filter facilities

3. **Browse Facilities**
   - Scroll through the grid of available courts
   - Each card shows:
     - Facility name (e.g., "Badminton Court 1")
     - Location on campus
     - Price per session
     - Facility image

![Placeholder: Home screen with facilities grid screenshot]

### Making a Booking

#### Step 1: Select a Facility

1. **Choose Your Court**
   - Tap on the facility card you want to book
   - You'll be taken to the booking page

#### Step 2: Configure Your Booking

1. **Review Facility Details**
   - Court name, location, and price are displayed at the top
   - View the location map to know where the facility is located

2. **Select Date**
   - Tap the date picker
   - Choose your preferred date (today or future dates)
   - Past dates are disabled

3. **Choose Time Slot**
   - View all available time slots for the selected date
   - Available slots are shown in a grid format
   - Tap on your preferred time slot
   - Selected slots are highlighted in blue
   - Unavailable slots are grayed out

![Placeholder: Booking page with date and time selection screenshot]

#### Step 3: Confirm Booking

1. **Review Your Selection**
   - Double-check the facility, date, and time
   - Verify the total cost

2. **Tap "Confirm Booking"**
   - The button is at the bottom of the screen
   - Wait for the confirmation process

#### Step 4: Provide Contact Details

After confirming, you'll be prompted to enter:
- **Contact Name**: Your full name
- **Student ID**: Your UTS student identification number
- **Phone Number**: Your contact number

![Placeholder: Contact details form screenshot]

#### Step 5: Booking Confirmation

1. **Review Final Details**
   - Check all booking information
   - Review the total cost
   - Confirm payment details

2. **Complete Booking**
   - Tap "Complete Booking" or "Proceed to Payment"
   - Wait for the success message

3. **Booking Success**
   - You'll see a confirmation screen
   - Your booking ID will be displayed
   - The booking is added to your schedule

![Placeholder: Booking success screen screenshot]

### Search Functionality

To quickly find a specific facility:

1. **Use the Search Bar**
   - Located at the top of the Home screen
   - Tap the search box

2. **Enter Keywords**
   - Type the sport name (e.g., "badminton")
   - Type facility name (e.g., "Court 1")
   - Type location keywords

3. **View Results**
   - Facilities matching your search appear instantly
   - Tap any result to proceed with booking

## 12. Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### Cannot Log In

**Problem**: Unable to sign in to your account

**Solutions**:
1. Verify you're using the correct email and password
2. Check your internet connection
3. Try the "Forgot Password" feature
4. Ensure you've completed account registration
5. Clear app cache and try again

#### Facilities Not Loading

**Problem**: Home screen shows no facilities or won't load

**Solutions**:
1. Check your internet connection
2. Pull down to refresh the page
3. Close and reopen the app
4. Check if you're logged in
5. Verify the app has internet permissions

#### Booking Fails

**Problem**: Unable to complete a booking

**Solutions**:
1. Ensure you have a stable internet connection
2. Verify the time slot is still available (refresh)
3. Check all required fields are filled correctly
4. Ensure you're logged into your account
5. Try selecting a different time slot

#### Time Slots Not Appearing

**Problem**: No time slots shown when trying to book

**Solutions**:
1. The facility may be fully booked for that date
2. Try selecting a different date
3. Refresh the page
4. Check if you've selected a valid future date

#### App Crashes or Freezes

**Problem**: App becomes unresponsive

**Solutions**:
1. Force close the app and reopen
2. Restart your device
3. Check for app updates
4. Ensure your device has sufficient storage
5. Clear app cache from device settings
6. Reinstall the app if the problem persists

#### Cannot See Booking History

**Problem**: History page is empty or not loading

**Solutions**:
1. You may not have any past bookings yet
2. Check your internet connection for syncing
3. Ensure you're logged into the correct account
4. Pull down to refresh
5. Check if bookings are showing in Schedule instead

#### Profile Picture Not Updating

**Problem**: Unable to change profile picture

**Solutions**:
1. Check internet connection
2. Ensure the image file is in a supported format (JPEG, PNG)
3. Verify the image size is not too large
4. Grant photo access permissions to the app
5. Try using a different image

## 13. FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: Is OCULUS SPORT free to use?**  
A: The app is free to download and use. However, booking sports facilities requires payment according to the facility's hourly rate.

**Q: Can I use the app offline?**  
A: You can view your profile and booking history offline. However, making new bookings and viewing real-time availability requires an internet connection.

**Q: Do I need a UTS email to register?**  
A: Yes, the app is designed for UTS students and staff. Registration requires a valid UTS email address.

### Booking Questions

**Q: How far in advance can I book a facility?**  
A: Booking availability varies by facility. Generally, you can book several weeks in advance. Check the date picker for available dates.

**Q: Can I book multiple time slots at once?**  
A: Currently, the app processes one booking at a time. To book multiple slots, complete each booking individually.

**Q: What happens if I arrive late to my booking?**  
A: Your booking time is reserved for you. However, late arrivals may result in reduced available time as the slot ends at the scheduled time.

**Q: Can I modify or cancel a booking?**  
A: Contact support for booking modifications or cancellations. Include your booking ID for faster assistance.

**Q: Will I receive a confirmation for my booking?**  
A: Yes, you'll see a confirmation screen after successful booking, and a notification will be sent to you.

### Payment Questions

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**  
A: Payment methods are displayed during the booking confirmation process. Common methods include credit/debit cards and online payment gateways.

**Q: Will I get a receipt?**  
A: Yes, booking receipts are stored in your History and can be accessed anytime.

**Q: What is the cancellation policy?**  
A: Cancellation policies vary. Please contact support with your booking details for specific policy information.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Which devices are supported?**  
A: The app supports Android devices (API 21+) and Windows 10+ computers.

**Q: Why does the app need internet permission?**  
A: Internet access is required to check real-time facility availability, process bookings, and sync your data with the server.

**Q: How do I update the app?**  
A: Updates are released periodically. Check the download source (app store or website) for the latest version.

**Q: Is my data secure?**  
A: Yes, the app uses Firebase Authentication for secure login and data storage. Your information is encrypted and protected.
